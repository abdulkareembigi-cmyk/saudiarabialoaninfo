Residency & Employment Requirements

Loan Eligibility for Expats & Citizens — شروط التأهل للمواطنين والمقيمين

Clear overview of criteria for resident expatriates / foreign workers in Saudi Arabia and Saudi citizens.

RESIDENT EXPATS & FOREIGN WORKERS

KSA Resident Expats / Foreign Workers

Working in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam & Across KSA

Non-Saudi residents living and working in Saudi Arabia with a valid Iqama and active employment can obtain personal and SME financing through SAMA-licensed banks and finance companies, subject to lender tenure and salary rules.

Length of Service (3 to 12+ Months): Many lenders require 3 to 6 months minimum service for salary-transfer loans and 6 to 12+ months (plus valid Iqama) for non-transfer or standard resident products.
Minimum Monthly Salary & GOSI: Typically starts from 3,500 SAR to 5,000+ SAR depending on the bank and product, backed by active GOSI registration for private-sector employees.
Employer Approval & Salary Transfer: Working for an approved employer or transferring your salary often improves profit rates, loan limits, and tenor options.
Credit Bureau Check (SIMAH): Applicants undergo a SIMAH credit check; strong payment history and low existing obligations support approval.
Required: Valid Iqama + Nafath Apply Now
SAUDI CITIZENS

Saudi Citizens (KSA Nationals)

Banking & SME Guarantee Programs

Saudi citizens have access to Shariah-compliant personal financing across Saudi banks and digital banks, alongside SME financing and loan guarantee programs such as Kafalah through partner banks.

Shariah-Compliant Financing: Personal Tawarruq / Murabaha options available across local and digital Saudi banks with transparent APR disclosures.
Employment & Salary Structure: Government, semi-government, and approved private-sector employees with GOSI/salary records qualify for standard tenors up to 60 months.
SAMA DBR Framework: Monthly debt commitments are assessed under SAMA responsible lending rules (33.33% standard consumer DBR cap, with higher brackets applying to specific mortgage programs).
SME Support & Kafalah: Small and medium enterprises can access bank financing supported by the Kafalah loan guarantee program and Monsha'at resources.
